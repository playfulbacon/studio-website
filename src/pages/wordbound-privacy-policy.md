**Effective date:** 2026-08-25<br />
**Data controller:** Kettle Games Inc.<br />
**Contact:** [contact@kettle.games](mailto:contact@kettle.games)

This policy describes what data Wordbound ("the game") collects, how it is
used, and your choices. It applies to Wordbound on all platforms.

## Data we collect

**Platform account information.** The game receives account information from
the platform you play on, which may include your platform user ID, your
platform username, and your profile image. Where a platform does not provide
account information, the game instead generates a random player name and
identifier on your device.

**Game content you create.** Your leaderboard scores, the word puzzles you
create in hide-and-seek mode, and the decoration of your in-game room.

**Local save data.** Your game progress (levels completed, items collected,
coins) is stored on your device or in your platform's save system. It is not
transmitted to us.

**Analytics data.** We use Unity Analytics to understand how the game is
played. It collects device information (such as device model, operating
system, country derived from your IP address, and a randomly generated
install identifier) and gameplay events (such as levels started and
completed, features used, and session length). Analytics data is not linked
to your platform account: your platform user ID and username are never
included in analytics events.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, messages, health data, or
browsing history, and the game does not serve ads.

## How we use it

Your platform user ID is used as your account key in our online database, so
your created content and scores stay yours across sessions and devices. Your
username is displayed with content you share — on leaderboards, on puzzles
you publish, and on your room when other players visit. Your profile image is
displayed only to you, on your own profile screen, and is not stored on our
servers.

Where your platform provides access to your friends list, it is used only
within the game session to show your friends' content (such as their puzzles)
first. Your friends list is never stored on our servers or shared.

Analytics data is used in aggregate to improve the game — for example, to
find levels that are too hard, features that go unused, or crashes. It is not
used to identify you, to build advertising profiles, or for any purpose
outside improving Wordbound.

Purchases of additional content are processed entirely by your platform. We
do not receive or store your payment details; we receive only confirmation of
what content your account owns, used to unlock it.

## Where your data is stored and who can access it

Your user ID, username, and created game content are stored in Firebase
Realtime Database, a hosting service operated by Google LLC in the United
States, acting as our data processor under the Firebase Data Processing and
Security Terms. Google does not use this data for its own purposes.

Analytics data is collected and processed for us by Unity Technologies
(Unity Analytics), acting as our data processor under Unity's data processing
terms, with processing in the United States and the European Union.

Content you share in the game (your username, scores, puzzles, and room) is
visible to other Wordbound players. We do not sell your data or share it with
third parties for advertising or profiling. We may disclose data if required
by law.

## Retention and deletion

Your online content is retained while your account remains active. You can
request deletion of all data associated with your account at any time by
emailing [contact@kettle.games](mailto:contact@kettle.games) from a means
that identifies your player account (e.g. your username); we will delete it
within 30 days.

Analytics data is retained by Unity Analytics according to its standard
retention period and can be deleted on request as part of an account data
deletion — though note analytics data cannot be tied back to your player
account, so deletion requests are fulfilled via the device identifier where
technically possible.

## Children

Wordbound does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13
(or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). If we learn we
have done so, we will delete it.

## Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective
date. Material changes will be highlighted in the game or on our website.
